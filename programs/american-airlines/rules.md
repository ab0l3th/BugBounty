# American Airlines Program Rules

## Approval and boundaries
- All testing must stay within official program scope and approval.
- Any activity outside the approved scope is prohibited.
- Passive recon is preferred unless active testing is explicitly approved.
- This program is a disclosure program, not a bounty program; no monetary reward is offered.

## Allowed activity
- Passive reconnaissance on approved assets
- Subdomain and public metadata discovery relevant to in-scope assets
- Documentation and reporting of approved findings
- Reporting of findings with a reproducible proof of concept and clear impact

## Prohibited activity
- Social engineering, phishing, vishing, or smishing
- Exploit validation without explicit approval
- Access to customer data or sensitive production data
- Credential harvesting, credential stuffing, or data exfiltration
- Testing of out-of-scope assets
- Denial-of-service or service degradation attempts
- MITM, physical access, or device tampering activity
- High-volume low-quality submissions

## Evidence and reporting
- Store only sanitized results and non-sensitive findings
- Document timestamps, scope, and testing context
- Maintain a clear record for reporting and review
- Keep reports limited to one vulnerability per submission unless a chained issue is necessary to demonstrate impact
- If uncertain about approval boundaries, pause and engage the program team before continuing
